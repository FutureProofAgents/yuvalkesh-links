# FutureProof landing-page content

Yuval's standing direction, 9 October 2026:

- Start every new or revised business landing page with a specific ideal customer, their recurring business problem, and the commercial value of solving it. Use relevant geography and service tier when known.
- Ground the offer in real FutureProof discovery, project scopes and client workflows. Explain the trigger, the work today, the concrete output, and what becomes faster, clearer or more profitable. Include a practical example the buyer recognizes.
- Construction example: premium NYC renovation/design-build firms serving high-end clients; a quote generator that combines approved pricing and scope with interior-design concepts and material options in one proposal.
- Apply the same specificity to every industry. Avoid generic AI capability lists as the main sales story.
- Explain speed and value honestly. Separate measured results, client-reported baselines, demos, proposed acceptance targets and illustrative calculations. Do not claim a demo time is a production guarantee or publish private client terms or identities without authorization.
- Use candid, realistic and context-specific imagery with natural people, places and materials. Generated scenes must not be presented as actual customers or testimonials.
- Keep page copy and requested companion Google Docs aligned. Register final pages and updates through the content-studio-log skill.
- Each solution headline must be a concise description of what the solution does (for example, AI quote generation with interior design sketches). Follow it with the buyer’s recognizable pain and state who the service is for, including firm type and buying role. Explain the practical business change before the technical detail.
- Organize the /pages/ catalog by sector so prospects can browse the solutions relevant to their business.
- Position FutureProof as a service that builds and implements agentic solutions in the client’s existing CRM and tools. Do not frame these offers as standalone products or self-serve subscriptions. Show relevant integration logos as examples, without implying partnerships or universal compatibility.
- Use the approved FutureProof workflow-infographic style for use-case covers: dark background, blue accents, existing logo and Inter type. Show the sector-specific inputs, agent preparation, human review and business output, labeled as an illustrative workflow. Maintain editable SVG sources and optimized PNG exports with separate wide catalog/email and portrait hero compositions; preserve full text without cropping and verify at actual display sizes.
